The Mission

Money skills are the part schools can actually do something about.

The Skyward Project is a youth-led research and policy effort on fairness in education, real money skills, and how ready students are for life after school.

The question

Among high-school students in Pune and Mumbai, how does financial literacy differ from one school board to another, and from one income group to another?

Why it matters

Everyone has to save, borrow, use UPI, spot a scam, and plan ahead — often long before anyone explains how it works. Just 27% of Indian adults are financially literate, against 52% in advanced economies.

Knowing how to send money is not the same as understanding debt, inflation, or interest.

No school is the default

Government, municipal, aided, low-fee private, mainstream private and vernacular-medium schools are each treated as their own group. Results are reported group by group, not blended into one average.

What we need

A first pilot of about three schools across different boards in Pune and Mumbai, and guidance on research ethics for minors.

If you can help, write to us.
