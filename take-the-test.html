Vol. I · Pune & Mumbai · 2026

For students

Take the online test.

Grades 9 to 12 · 27 questions · emailed results

This paper is for ninth to twelfth graders who want to know where they stand on money skills, and what they must improve on. It is the same FinLit Index we use in the field — written for a sixteen-year-old in Pune or Mumbai.

How long it takes

It is a relatively short test of 27 questions. Most students finish in under 15 minutes. We have given you 30.

What you get afterwards

After you submit, we will send you a direct email with your score, the questions you got wrong, the correct option, and an explanation of why that option is right. Use an email address you can open on this device or later the same day. Check spam if it does not arrive within a few minutes.

Before you start

  • 27 questions

    25 marks, four parts. Warm up, everyday money, careful reasoning, then a short market-awareness round.

  • One answer each

    You can move between questions until you submit, or until the 30 minutes end.

  • Your email

    Required, so we can send your score and the explanations. We do not put the answer key on the test page itself.

Financial decisions in real life still depend on your family, your income, and your goals. This test does not tell you what is right for your household. It measures how you reason about money as a general skill.

Schools hosting a research sitting should write to us rather than using this public page.
