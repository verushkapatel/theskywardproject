The Skyward Project

Financial literacy should not be a privilege tied to school type, family background, or academic stream.

What we do

A youth-led research and policy initiative on educational equity, financial literacy, and how ready students are for life after school.

How we measure it

The FinLit Index. One forty-minute paper, adapted from the S&P Global Financial Literacy Survey, sat in identical form by every student from Grade 9 to Grade 12.

Where we are

Pune and Mumbai, 2026. The instrument is written. We are looking for a first pilot across schools of different boards and income groups in both cities.
