The FinLit Index

One paper, every grade, every board.

A forty-minute assessment, adapted from a global benchmark and rewritten for a sixteen-year-old in Pune or Mumbai. Every result is read against the board a student sits under and the household they come from.

The benchmark

Adapted from the S&P Global Financial Literacy Survey — four ideas: numeracy, compound interest, inflation, and spreading risk. We keep each idea at the same difficulty and rebuild it as a situation from a student's own life.

The paper

Forty minutes. Scenario-based multiple choice. The difficulty climbs across four tiers: foundation, developing, proficient, and advanced.

One paper, one answer key, three languages — English, Hindi and Marathi. No school is named or ranked.

Schools interested in a pilot can get in touch.
